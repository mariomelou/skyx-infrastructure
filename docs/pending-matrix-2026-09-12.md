## Bottom line

The inventory points (security groups, routes, endpoint state and policy, autoscaling state, deployment circuit-breaker state, ALB listener/rule/target-group-health/attribute state, alarms, Config, WAF, and the three main application IAM roles) are documented as evidence. They are not yet broadly imported or managed by CloudFormation. The first adoption candidate was scoped as one ECR repository, with `skyx-backend` recommended; its import-only change set was reviewed, explicitly approved, and executed successfully. Aurora protection, ECS autoscaling, ECS circuit breaker, and WAF COUNT were then applied directly and verified in the AWS Console; their CDK adoption remains pending. The remaining work is to configure the protected GitHub environment, resolve SNS/on-call, hostname/certificate, network-egress, alarm-threshold, and retention inputs, and separately approve each hardening or ownership change described in `docs/hardening-plan-2026-09-12.md`.
